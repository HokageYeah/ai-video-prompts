# 猫结者 - 第一个场景脚本

> 场景编号：Scene 01
> 场景主题：狸花猫公仔在厨房进食时遭遇外星机器人袭击
> 时长：10 秒
> 目标平台：通用高质量视频模型
> 默认风格：迪士尼/皮克斯 3D 动画
> 术语参考：`styles.md` Style 1 + `glossary.md`

## 中文解析版

### 1. Visual Style & Rendering

采用迪士尼/皮克斯式高完成度 3D 动画视觉，完整沿用风格关键词：`Pixar-quality 3D animation, subsurface scattering skin, exaggerated cartoon proportions, vibrant saturated colors, soft volumetric lighting, physically-based cloth simulation, expressive facial rig`。画幅使用 **2.39:1** 电影宽银幕，镜头语言参考 **35mm spherical, deep depth of field, slight barrel distortion**。整体渲染采用 **Pixar RenderMan + Path Tracing + SSS (Subsurface Scattering) + Ambient Occlusion**，强调毛绒公仔触感与科幻闯入感的对撞。午后厨房前半段维持温暖高饱和度基调，主色为奶油白 `#FFF6E8`、焦糖棕 `#B9753F`、木质浅金 `#D8A36A`；机器人出现后引入冰蓝 `#00D7FF` 与警示红 `#FF4A4A`。色温从前 4 秒的 **6100K** 暖调，在机器人现身瞬间于 **6 帧内**骤降到 **4300K**，冷光从画面后方 35° 方向切入，不锈钢与瓷砖边缘出现冷色反射。猫咪全身约 **32,000 根**毛发做 strand-level hair/fur simulation，炸毛过渡控制在 **9 帧 / 0.375 秒**，激光命中时附带 **180 个**高亮火星粒子与轻微 HDR highlight rolloff。

### 2. Characters & Props

**狸花猫公仔「花卷」**：
- 体格/体型：头身比 **1:1.4**，体长约 **22cm**，重量感约 **2.1kg**
- 表面材质：主毛色焦糖棕 `#B9753F`，虎斑纹深咖 `#3A2418`，腹部与嘴边奶油白 `#FFF8F0`，采用 stylized PBR + SSS 表现毛绒纤维感
- 面部/核心视觉：大圆眼虹膜琥珀橙 `#FF9A24`，高光为圆形湿润高光，鼻头樱粉 `#F7A8B8`
- 标志性特征：脸颊绒毛厚、尾巴末端蓬松，受惊后形成夸张蒲公英式炸开轮廓

**常态**：花卷蹲在陶瓷猫粮碗前，前爪内收，尾巴绕右侧半圈，眼睛半眯，咀嚼频率约 **2 次/秒**，耳朵自然前倾，胡须轻微起伏。  
**变化态**：发现身后威胁的 **0.4 秒**内，头部猛地向后扭转 **85°**，瞳孔扩大 **2.2x**，背部弓起，身体后缩 **14cm**，全身毛发以鼻尖到尾尖的顺序级联炸开，尾巴膨胀 **2.6x**。  
**极端态**：进入逃命状态后，四肢拉长，身体水平方向 stretch 至 **1.8x**，耳朵贴平，嘴巴张成方形惊叫口型，尾部毛发被高速运动压成向后流动的尖束。

**外星机器人「Z-7」**：
- 体格/体型：高约 **95cm**，圆角胶囊型躯干，重量感约 **18kg**
- 表面材质：钛灰金属 `#99A7B5`，冷白磨砂外壳 `#DDE6EE`，PBR 金属粗糙度约 **0.42**
- 面部/核心视觉：单目扫描镜为冰蓝 `#00D7FF`，锁定时中心切换警示红 `#FF4A4A`
- 标志性特征：右臂可折叠展开激光枪，枪口内核为高能琥珀黄 `#FFC247`

**常态**：从厨房后方阴影区无声滑入，头部扫描环缓慢旋转。  
**变化态**：锁定目标时单目收缩为十字准星，右臂抬升 **60°**，枪口蓄能 **0.8 秒**，胸口指示灯闪烁频率提升到 **4Hz**。  
**极端态**：连续射击落空后身体前倾，脚底推进器喷出短促蓝白尾焰，金属关节出现急促抖动。

**猫粮碗**：
- 材质：白釉陶瓷 `#F8F8F4`，内壁浅蓝 `#DDEFFF`，碗口直径 **12cm**
- 事件前状态：装有约 **28 颗**棕色猫粮，碗边散落 3 颗
- 事件后状态：被激光擦中后边缘形成 **2.8cm** 焦痕，随后被猫咪逃跑时带翻，旋转 **2.5 圈** 后停下

### 3. Environment & Scene

场景设定在家庭厨房的午后时段，左侧窗户射入柔和太阳光，阳光以 **48°** 俯角穿过空气中的灰尘形成明显 Tyndall Effect。地面为暖米色哑光瓷砖 `#F4E7D3`，墙柜是奶白色 `#FFF9F0`，台面为浅灰石英石 `#D6D6D6`。前景可见桌角与一只玻璃水杯，增强空间纵深；远处微波炉和不锈钢水槽落在轻微景深虚化中，光圈感参考 **f/2.8**。空气中约 **120 个**可见浮尘粒子在光束中缓慢漂移，机器人入场后，后景冷蓝光让粒子从金黄反射切换成银白闪点。

**环境音效层**：基础层包括猫粮咀嚼声、冰箱低频嗡鸣、窗外很轻的城市环境底噪；事件触发层包括机器人关节伺服轻鸣、激光蓄能高频上升音、瓷碗摩擦地面声、猫爪急停刮擦声。音量曲线从开场安静舒适，转入威胁出现时的低频压迫，再在追击阶段迅速拉满。

**情绪转折环境变化**：前半段是暖光安全区，机器人出现后厨房后景阴影被冷蓝光填充，台面金属反射变硬，猫咪逃跑经过时带起浮尘和猫粮颗粒，形成明显二级运动。

### 4. Physics & VFX

猫咪炸毛使用 **Hair/Fur Simulation + Spring-Damper System**，确保每束毛发都有 **0.02 秒** 的级联延迟；身体后缩时加入 **Soft Body Deformation**，让毛绒公仔拥有可爱的挤压与回弹。机器人激光枪发射采用细束高能脉冲，单次激光束直径约 **3cm**，伴随 **neon bloom** 感的边缘辉光和 **24 个**拖尾粒子。激光擦过地面与瓷碗时触发 **Particle System**，火花沿抛物线飞散 **10-18cm**，并留下短暂发红焦痕。猫咪闪避过程中四爪与地面碰撞具有明确 collision response，急转弯时身体出现 cartoon-style squash & stretch，尾巴与胡须存在二级运动。签名镜头将出现在第一次激光擦身而过时，加入 **0.35x time dilation** 慢动作回放，展示激光光束掠过炸开的毛尖、毛发边缘的光散射和粒子拖尾。

### 5. Camera Work

开场使用缓慢 **Dolly In**，从中景推进到花卷吃猫粮的近中景，营造松弛生活感。机器人现身时执行轻微 **Rack Focus**，将焦点从前景猫咪切到背景冷光中的 Z-7，再迅速拉回猫咪的反应。猫咪炸毛瞬间加入 **Dutch Angle 10°** 强化惊吓。机器人开火后镜头切入低机位 **Tracking Shot** 跟随猫咪左右闪避，再配合一次短促 **Whip Pan** 展现射击方向变化。结尾以略微手持感的横向跟拍，让猫咪冲出画面右侧，镜头停在狼藉厨房与仍在瞄准的机器人之间。

### 6. Action Timeline

**[00:00-00:02] 午后嚼嚼（铺垫）**  
花卷在阳光里的猫粮碗前悠闲进食，头部轻点，耳朵自然摆动，3 颗猫粮碎屑沿碗边弹跳 **2-3cm**。镜头缓慢推进，厨房暖光稳定，环境音安静柔和。

**[00:02-00:04] 冷影潜入（预警）**  
机器人 Z-7 从猫咪身后阴影区滑入，单目冰蓝扫描环在背景亮起，焦点短暂切到后景。猫咪尚未完全察觉，但左耳先轻抖一次，胡须出现 **0.1 秒** 的不安抽动。

**[00:04-00:05.5] 毛刺觉醒（觉醒）**  
花卷猛然回头，身体后缩进入作战姿态。毛发按鼻尖到尾尖顺序爆炸式竖起，尾巴膨胀成大号蓬球，瞳孔迅速放大。微互动包括猫粮碗被后爪碰得滑移 **4cm**，两颗猫粮滚入阳光光斑。

**[00:05.5-00:07] 激光擦身（爆发） [Signature Shot]**  
Z-7 抬起激光枪完成锁定并射出第一发脉冲。正常速度下，花卷向右侧猛扑躲开，激光擦过碗沿打出焦痕与火花。随后立刻插入 **0.35x** 慢动作回放：镜头 Rack Focus 从前景飞溅火星切到猫咪炸开的毛尖，显示光束从毛发边缘旁掠过，粒子拖尾与微小热扰动在空气中扩散，形成本场最强视觉奇观点。

**[00:07-00:09] 之字逃窜（追逐）**  
机器人连续追射两次，猫咪以左右之字形高速闪避，四爪在瓷砖上刮出短促滑痕。尾巴、胡须和脸颊绒毛出现明显二级运动；被掀翻的猫粮碗旋转滑开，猫粮颗粒散落半径约 **35cm**。

**[00:09-00:10] 逃出画面（收束）**  
花卷最后一次弹射冲刺，从画面右侧冲出，只留下被气流带动的浮尘和轻微晃动的窗边挂布。机器人停在厨房中央，枪口仍有一缕琥珀余辉，环境音从混乱迅速跌回冰箱嗡鸣与滚动猫粮的尾音。

## English Prompt (Copy-Ready)

### 1. Visual Style & Rendering

`Pixar-quality 3D animation, subsurface scattering skin, exaggerated cartoon proportions, vibrant saturated colors, soft volumetric lighting, physically-based cloth simulation, expressive facial rig`. Cinematic **2.39:1** widescreen, using **35mm spherical, deep depth of field, slight barrel distortion**. Render with **Pixar RenderMan, Path Tracing, SSS, Ambient Occlusion**, emphasizing plush fur tactility colliding with a sudden sci-fi threat. Opening palette: cream white `#FFF6E8`, caramel brown `#B9753F`, warm wood gold `#D8A36A`; threat palette: ice blue `#00D7FF`, alert red `#FF4A4A`. Color temperature starts at **6100K** and drops to **4300K** within **6 frames** when the alien robot appears from the rear shadow at a **35°** light angle. The cat uses approximately **32,000 fur strands** with strand-level hair simulation, full bristle transition in **9 frames / 0.375s**. Laser impact adds **180 spark particles**, subtle HDR highlight rolloff, and reflective cold spill on metal and tile.

### 2. Characters & Props

**Tabby Cat Plush "Huajuan"**  
Build: head-to-body ratio **1:1.4**, body length **22cm**, weight feel **2.1kg**. Fur colors: caramel brown `#B9753F`, dark stripe brown `#3A2418`, cream muzzle and belly `#FFF8F0`, rendered with stylized PBR + SSS. Huge amber eyes `#FF9A24`, soft wet circular specular highlight, pink nose `#F7A8B8`. Signature trait: thick cheek fluff and a fluffy tail tip that expands into a dandelion-like silhouette when frightened.  
Resting state: crouched at the bowl, chewing at **2 bites per second**, ears forward, tail wrapped to the right.  
Change state: in **0.4s**, snaps head backward **85°**, pupils expand **2.2x**, spine arches, body recoils **14cm**, fur cascades upright from nose to tail, tail volume expands **2.6x**.  
Extreme state: during escape, limbs stretch, body elongates to **1.8x**, ears flatten, mouth opens into a square panic scream, tail fur streams backward.

**Alien Robot "Z-7"**  
Height **95cm**, rounded capsule body, weight feel **18kg**. Titanium gray `#99A7B5` and matte cold shell `#DDE6EE`, PBR roughness **0.42**. Main eye glows ice blue `#00D7FF`, switching to alert red `#FF4A4A` when locked on. Right arm unfolds into a laser gun with an amber core `#FFC247`.  
Resting state: silently glides from the dark rear kitchen area, scanner ring rotating slowly.  
Change state: eye contracts into a targeting crosshair, right arm raises **60°**, weapon charges for **0.8s**, chest indicator blinks at **4Hz**.  
Extreme state: after missed shots, the body leans forward, foot thrusters emit short blue-white bursts, joints vibrate in frustration.

**Ceramic Kibble Bowl**  
White glaze `#F8F8F4`, pale blue interior `#DDEFFF`, **12cm** diameter, about **28 visible kibbles** plus 3 spilled pieces. After the laser graze, a **2.8cm** scorch mark appears on the rim, then the bowl gets knocked over and spins **2.5 turns**.

### 3. Environment & Scene

A cozy home kitchen in the afternoon. Soft sunlight enters from the left window at a **48°** downward angle, creating visible Tyndall light shafts through drifting dust. Floor material: warm matte beige tile `#F4E7D3`; cabinets: creamy white `#FFF9F0`; countertop: pale gray quartz `#D6D6D6`. Foreground includes a table corner and a glass cup for depth; microwave and stainless-steel sink sit in mild DOF blur around **f/2.8**. About **120 visible dust particles** drift inside the light beam. When the robot enters, the rear cool-blue light shifts the particles from golden glints to silver-white sparkles. Ambient audio includes cat chewing, low fridge hum, and faint exterior city noise; triggered audio includes servo whine, rising laser charge tone, ceramic scraping, and claw skids.

### 4. Physics & VFX

Use **Hair/Fur Simulation + Spring-Damper System** with **0.02s** cascade delay per fur cluster. Add **Soft Body Deformation** for plush recoil and bounce. The laser is a thin high-energy pulse, beam diameter about **3cm**, with bloom and **24 trailing particles**. Laser strikes create a **Particle System** of sparks scattering **10-18cm** in parabolic arcs and leaving brief red-hot scorch marks. The cat's paws have clear collision response on tile, with cartoon squash-and-stretch during hard turns. The signature shot uses **0.35x time dilation**, showing the laser skimming past exploded fur tips with rack focus, particle trails, and light scattering through the airborne debris.

### 5. Camera Work

Open with a gentle **Dolly In** from medium shot into a medium close-up of the cat eating. When the robot appears, use a quick **Rack Focus** from the foreground cat to the background cold-lit Z-7, then snap back to the cat's reaction. Apply a **Dutch Angle 10°** at the bristle moment. During laser fire, shift into a low-angle **Tracking Shot** following the cat's left-right evasive movement, with one sharp **Whip Pan** to reveal the changing attack direction. End with a slightly handheld lateral follow as the cat exits frame right, leaving the camera on the messy kitchen and the still-aiming robot.

### 6. Action Timeline

**[00:00-00:02] Afternoon Crunch (Setup)**  
Huajuan calmly eats kibble in a warm sun patch. Tiny kibble crumbs bounce **2-3cm** along the bowl rim. Camera slowly pushes in, atmosphere feels safe and domestic.

**[00:02-00:04] Cold Shadow Entry (Warning)**  
Z-7 glides into the rear background shadow. Its ice-blue scanner eye lights up. The cat does not fully notice yet, but one ear twitches and the whiskers show a **0.1s** nervous tremor.

**[00:04-00:05.5] Fur Awakening (Awakening)**  
The cat snaps its head back and recoils into a defensive posture. Fur erupts upright from nose to tail, tail blooms into a huge puffball, pupils dilate rapidly. Secondary interaction: the rear paw nudges the bowl **4cm**, sending two kibbles rolling into the sunlit patch.

**[00:05.5-00:07] Laser Near-Miss (Burst) [Signature Shot]**  
Z-7 raises the laser gun, locks on, and fires the first pulse. At normal speed, the cat lunges right and the beam grazes the bowl rim, throwing sparks. Immediately insert a **0.35x** slow-motion replay: rack focus from flying sparks to the exploding fur tips as the beam passes just beside them, with motion trails, micro heat distortion, and suspended particles.

**[00:07-00:09] Zigzag Escape (Chase)**  
The robot fires two more times while the cat dodges in fast left-right zigzags. Claws scratch the tile, whiskers and tail show strong secondary motion, and the flipped bowl spins away while kibble scatters across a **35cm** radius.

**[00:09-00:10] Exit Frame (Resolve)**  
The cat performs one final spring-loaded dash and exits frame right. Dust hangs in the disturbed air, a curtain edge trembles, and the robot remains in the kitchen center with a fading amber glow at the gun barrel. Audio collapses back to fridge hum and one last rolling kibble.
